## Download

| Your computer | Download |
| --- | --- |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 and later) | `Highlighter-…-macos-apple-silicon.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Highlighter-…-macos-intel.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Highlighter-…-windows-x64.zip` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Click the Apple menu → **About This Mac**. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon; "Processor: … Intel" means Intel.

Everything Highlighter needs (yt-dlp, ffmpeg, deno) is included. Nothing else to install.

## Install on macOS

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Highlighter** onto **Applications**.
2. Open Highlighter from your Applications folder. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the developer: click **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**, then confirm. You only do this once.
3. Highlighter opens in your browser. It runs in the background; click **Quit** at the top right of the page to stop it, and open the app again to get back to it.

## Install on Windows

1. Right-click the `.zip` → **Extract All…** → **Extract**.
2. Open the extracted **Highlighter** folder and double-click **Highlighter.exe**. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Highlighter opens in your browser. Keep the black console window open while you use it; click **Quit** in the page (or close that window) to stop.

Full instructions and troubleshooting: see the [README](https://github.com/krismp/highlighter#install).
