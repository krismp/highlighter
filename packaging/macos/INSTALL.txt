HIGHLIGHTER FOR macOS
=====================

1. Double-click the .dmg file in this zip to open it.

2. Drag "Highlighter" onto the "Applications" folder in the window that opens.

3. Open your Applications folder and double-click Highlighter.

   The first time, macOS says it cannot verify the developer, because the
   app is not from the App Store. Click "Done", then:

     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down to "Highlighter was blocked..." and click "Open Anyway".
     - Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

   You only do this once.

Highlighter opens in its own window. Close the window (or press Cmd+Q, or
click "Quit" at the top right) to stop it.

Everything Highlighter needs (yt-dlp, ffmpeg, deno) is inside the app.
Nothing else to install. Clips are saved to Movies > Highlights
(changeable in Settings).

Still blocked? Open Terminal and run:
    xattr -cr /Applications/Highlighter.app
then open the app again.
