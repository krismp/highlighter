HIGHLIGHTER FOR macOS
=====================

1. Drag "Highlighter" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Highlighter.

   The first time, macOS says it cannot verify the developer, because the
   app is not from the App Store. Click "Done" (or "Cancel"), then:

     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down to "Highlighter was blocked..." and click "Open Anyway".
     - Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open Anyway" again.

   You only do this once.

3. Highlighter opens in your web browser at http://127.0.0.1:7777.
   It has no window of its own: it runs quietly in the background.

   - To get back to it, open Highlighter again (it reopens the browser tab).
   - To stop it, click "Quit" in the top-right corner of the page.

Everything highlighter needs (yt-dlp, ffmpeg, deno) is inside the app.
Nothing else to install.

Clips are saved to Movies > Highlights (changeable in Settings).
If something goes wrong, the log is at ~/Library/Logs/Highlighter.log.

Still blocked? Open Terminal and run:
    xattr -cr /Applications/Highlighter.app
then open the app again.
